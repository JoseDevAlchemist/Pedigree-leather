An order is accepted for processing after its details and availability have been confirmed. Payment methods available at checkout are those currently offered by Pedigree Leather.

## 3. Customer cancellations, including paid orders

**Customers may request cancellation at any time before delivery has been confirmed as completed**, even when the order has already been paid for.

Cancellation requests may be made through our official **WhatsApp contact** or **customer-support email**, as displayed on the Pedigree Leather website. Please provide your order number and enough details for us to locate the order.

Once the cancellation is confirmed, we will stop fulfilment where possible and arrange a refund of any eligible payment already received, using an appropriate payment method and in accordance with applicable law. If delivery is already in progress, we may need to contact the delivery team to prevent completion. We will communicate the status to you.

Once successful delivery has been confirmed, the Returns & Exchanges Policy below applies, without affecting any additional cancellation or other rights provided by law.

Before making a delivery, **Pedigree Leather will normally call the customer** using the contact number provided with the order to confirm that they or an authorized recipient will be available to receive it.

Customers are responsible for providing correct contact information, delivery addresses and reasonable access instructions. We will communicate delivery charges and available delivery options during checkout or order confirmation.

Delivery timelines are estimates unless expressly agreed otherwise. If circumstances delay or prevent delivery, we will inform the customer and work toward an appropriate resolution.

Customers are encouraged to inspect their items **at the time of delivery or collection**, checking the product, colour, size, visible condition and completeness against the order.

For in-store purchases, our cashier or store staff will inspect the goods before handover, and customers are encouraged to check them as well.

Inspection at handover helps identify visible issues promptly. **It does not take away a customer's rights concerning hidden or manufacturing defects** that could not reasonably have been detected at the time.

## 6. Returns and exchanges

### 6.1 Final sales and change of mind

As a general store policy, **goods once sold are not returnable or exchangeable solely because of a change of mind, a change in preference, or an incorrectly selected size or colour**, except where Pedigree Leather agrees otherwise or where applicable law gives the customer a right to a remedy.

This policy applies to **bags, shoes, belts and other products**. Customers should check shoe size and fit, where possible, before purchase.

### 6.2 Damaged, defective or incorrect goods

If an item arrives **damaged, defective, incomplete or different from what was ordered**, please contact us as soon as possible, preferably **within three (3) calendar days of delivery or collection**.

To help us investigate, provide the order number or other reasonable proof of purchase, an explanation of the problem and, where available, photographs or video showing the issue. We may request an inspection of the item. We will consider whether the problem was present at handover or resulted from a manufacturing defect, delivery error or other cause.

**The three-day period is our requested reporting window, not an absolute cut-off for rights or remedies provided by Kenyan law.** A genuine defect that is discovered later will be considered in accordance with applicable legal protections.

### 6.3 Where Pedigree Leather is responsible

Where we supplied an incorrect product, an incomplete order, or an item that was damaged or defective at handover, **Pedigree Leather will take responsibility for correcting the order without charging the customer for the necessary return collection or replacement delivery**.

Depending on the nature of the issue and applicable law, the resolution may be a correction, repair, replacement or refund. We will explain the agreed resolution and arrange the necessary logistics at our cost where the mistake or qualifying defect is ours.

### 6.4 Damage arising after handover

Damage caused after handover by accidents, misuse, inappropriate handling, unauthorized alterations or ordinary wear and tear is not automatically considered a pre-existing or manufacturing defect. Each reported issue will be assessed fairly in light of the available evidence and applicable law.

### 6.5 Refunds

Where a refund is due following an accepted cancellation, a verified order error or a legal entitlement, we will process it through an appropriate payment channel and communicate its status. Processing times may depend on the payment provider and applicable legal requirements.
